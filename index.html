Frequently Asked Questions

Can self-employed people get a mortgage?

Yes. Lenders work with freelancers, business owners, and gig workers every day. The catch is that they calculate your income differently — usually using a 2-year average from your tax returns, not your current monthly earnings.

How is my income calculated for a mortgage?

Most lenders take your adjusted gross income from the last two tax returns, average it, and divide by 12. If your income grew, they may use the lower year to be safe. Our Self-Employed Calculator does this math for you.

What are the hidden costs of buying a home?

Property taxes, you homeowners insurance, HOA fees, and PM planI can add hundreds of dollars to your monthly payment. Our True Monthly Payment Calculator shows the full picture — not just principal and interest.

What are closing costs and how much are they?

Closing costs are extra fees due when you finalize a home purchase — usually 2–5% of the home price. They include loan origination, appraisal, title insurance, attorney fees, and prepaid taxes. Our Closing Costs Calculator breaks down every fee.

Is it cheaper to rent or buy?

It depends on your market, how to stay, and how much you put down. Our Rent vs Buy Calculator shows the true monthly cost of owning — including maintenance and taxes — and compares it to your rent.

How much should I put down on a house?

20% avoids PMI (private mortgage insurance) and gets you better rates. But FHA loans allow as little as 3.5%. Our Down Payment Calculator shows how long it takes to save and how much PMI you'd avoid.

When should I refinance my mortgage?

Usually when you can lower your rate enough to save more than the closing costs within the time you plan to stay. Our Refinance Calculator shows your monthly savings and the break-even point.

Can I get a mortgage with a low credit score?

Yes. FHA loans allow scores as low as 580 with 3.5% down. If your score is lower or you have no credit history, there are still options. Check our Thin Credit Calculator to see what fits your situation.

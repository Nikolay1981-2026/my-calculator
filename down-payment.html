How Much Down Payment Do You Need?

The down payment is usually the biggest hurdle for first-time homebuyers. While 20% is the gold standard, it's not a strict rule. Here is a breakdown of the most common down payment options:

Down PaymentLoan TypePros & Cons
3.5%FHA LoanLowest entry point, but requires mortgage insurance (MIP) for the life of the loan.
5% - 10%Conventional LoanGood balance. PMI is required until you reach 20% equity, but it can be removed later.
20%Conventional LoanThe sweet spot. You avoid PMI entirely, saving you hundreds per month.

What is PMI?

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is a fee lenders charge if your down payment is less than 20%. It protects the lender if you default on the loan. PMI usually costs between 0.5% and 1% of your loan amount per year. For a $350,000 loan, that's an extra $145 to $290 added to your monthly payment.

How to Save for a Down Payment Faster

  • Set a timeline: Use the calculator above to see exactly how many months you need to save.
  • Automate your savings: Set up an automatic transfer from your checking to a high-yield savings account on payday.
  • Cut one big expense: Instead of trying to save on everything, cut one major expense (like a car payment or a premium subscription) and put that money directly into savings.
  • Look into down payment assistance: Many states and cities offer grants or low-interest loans for first-time buyers.

Down Payment — FAQ

Is 20% down payment required?

No. It's a myth. Many loan programs allow down payments as low as 3% (conventional) or 3.5% (FHA). However, putting 20% down saves you from paying PMI.

Can I use gift money for a down payment?

Yes, most lenders allow you to use gift money from family members. You will just need a "gift letter" signed by the donor stating that the money does not need to be repaid.

How do I avoid PMI with a low down payment?

You can look into "piggyback" loans (like an 80-10-10 loan), where you take out a second mortgage to cover part of the down payment, avoiding PMI. Or, you can wait until you reach 20% equity and request PMI removal.

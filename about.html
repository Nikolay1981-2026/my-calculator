About This Project

Free, honest mortgage tools for people the banks usually overlook.

Why this site exists

Most mortgage calculators are built for people with a steady 9-to-5 salary, perfect credit, and a simple financial picture. But millions of Americans don't fit that mold — freelancers, gig workers, business owners, first-time buyers with thin credit, and anyone staring at the real cost of a home for the first time.

This site gives you straight answers. No sign-up walls. No pushy sales calls. Just calculators that do the math the way lenders actually do it.

What you'll find here

  • Self-Employed Mortgage Calculator — figures out how much you can borrow using your 2-year average income, the way lenders really calculate it for freelancers.
  • True Monthly Payment Calculator — shows the full picture, including property taxes, insurance, HOA fees, and PMI. Most calculators hide these costs.
  • Thin Credit Mortgage Calculator — helps buyers with low or no credit history understand what options are actually available.

How we calculate

All tools use standard mortgage formulas and industry benchmarks: the 43% debt-to-income (DTI) rule, the standard amortization formula, FHA and conventional loan guidelines, and current average rates for each credit tier. Results are estimates — your real rate depends on your lender, your location, and your full financial picture.

No data collection

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored, or shared. No accounts. No tracking of your inputs.

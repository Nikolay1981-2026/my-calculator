Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I start?

Start with our Home Affordability Calculator. It shows you the price range you can realistically afford based on your income and debts. Then explore other calculators to fine-tune your plan.

Can self-employed people get a mortgage?

Yes. Lenders work with freelancers, business owners, and gig workers every day. The catch is that they calculate your income differently — usually using a 2-year average from your tax returns. Our Self-Employed Calculator does this math for you.

How is my income calculated for a mortgage?

Most lenders take your adjusted gross income from the last two tax returns, average it, and divide by 12. If your income grew, they may use the lower year to be safe.

What are the hidden costs of buying a home?

Property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, and PMI can add hundreds of dollars to your monthly payment. Our True Monthly Payment Calculator shows the full picture.

What are closing costs and how much are they?

Closing costs are extra fees due when you finalize a home purchase — usually 2–5% of the home price. Our Closing Costs Calculator breaks down every fee.

Is it cheaper to rent or buy?

It depends on your market, how long you plan to stay, and how much you put down. Our Rent vs Buy Calculator shows the true monthly cost of owning — including maintenance and taxes.

How much should I put down on a house?

20% avoids PMI and gets you better rates. But FHA loans allow as little as 3.5%. Our Down Payment Calculator shows how long it takes to save.

How much are property taxes?

Property tax rates range from 0.28% (Hawaii) to 1.85% (New Jersey) of your home's value per year. Our Property Tax Calculator shows the rate for your state.

When should I refinance my mortgage?

Usually when you can lower your rate enough to save more than the closing costs within the time you plan to stay. Our Refinance Calculator shows your break-even point.

How can I pay off my mortgage faster?

Even small extra payments make a huge difference. Our Mortgage Payoff Calculator shows how much time and interest you save with extra monthly payments.

Can I get a mortgage with a low credit score?

Yes. FHA loans allow scores as low as 580 with 3.5% down. Check our Thin Credit Calculator to see what fits your situation.
